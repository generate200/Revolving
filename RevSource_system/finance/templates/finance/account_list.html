📋 Understanding Account Balances

Key Principle: In double-entry bookkeeping, every transaction affects TWO different accounts to keep the system balanced.

✓ Correct Entry Example

Transaction: Owner invests $5,000 cash
DEBIT: A1000 (Cash) - $5,000
CREDIT: E3000 (Owner Capital) - $5,000

Result: Cash ↑ $5,000, Owner Capital ↑ $5,000

✗ Common Mistake

Wrong: Debit A1000 + Credit A1000
Result: No change ($5,000 in, $5,000 out)

Always use TWO DIFFERENT accounts!
